Use icon-only controls for familiar repeated actions. Keep primary and unfamiliar actions as words. Disabled and busy are both inert, and they do not look alike: disabled dims the glyph, busy sits in a sunken ringed well and replaces the glyph with a spinner at full strength, and carries aria-busy. The well and the swapped glyph are static, so the difference survives prefers-reduced-motion, where the spin does not render. The label still names the action that is pending — the export above reads "Export companies" while it waits. phx-disable-with does not belong here: it replaces the control's text, which on an icon-only action deletes the glyph.
