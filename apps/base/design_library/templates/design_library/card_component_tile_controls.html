A workspace tile has no title bar: the page's own heading names it. Its controls float over the top right corner as one flat row: move, which drags the tile onto another to swap them, flip the split direction, and close. A focused tile keeps them in sight; on any other tile they appear when the pointer is over it or focus reaches them, so hover the second example. Every other tile operation is a key of the tiling mode.
